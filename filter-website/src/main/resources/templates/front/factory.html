How We Make

Manufacturing Process

01
Raw Material Inspection

Every batch of fiber, yarn and base cloth is inspected for weight, tensile strength and chemical composition before production.

02
Felt & Fabric Production

Advanced needle looms and air-jet weaving machines produce filter media with precise control of weight, thickness and permeability.

03
Cutting, Sewing & Finishing

CNC cutting ensures dimensional accuracy. Automated sewing lines guarantee seam quality. Post-treatment includes PTFE membrane, singeing and calendering.

04
QC & Shipping

100% inspection of dimensions, weight and appearance. Qualified products are professionally packed in PE bags and cartons for safe global delivery.
