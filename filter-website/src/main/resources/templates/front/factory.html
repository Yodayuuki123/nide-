Sewing & Assembly

Filter Bag Production

Our automated sewing lines use proprietary heat-welding technology to produce filter bags with superior seam strength.

  • Automated CNC cutting for precise dimensions
  • Heat-welded seams for maximum durability
  • Multi-needle industrial sewing machines
  • Capacity: 100,000+ bags per month
Felt & Fabric

Needle Felt Production

Advanced needle looms and air-jet weaving machines produce filter media with precise control of weight, thickness and air permeability.

  • Needle punch felts: PPS, P84, PTFE, Aramid, Fiberglass
  • Woven filter cloths for press filter applications
  • Controlled weight, thickness and permeability
  • Annual output: 10 million+ sqm of filter media
Raw Materials

Special Material Processing

From acrylic and carbon fiber to PTFE membrane lamination, our facility processes a full range of filtration materials.

  • Acrylic fiber processing for moderate temperatures
  • Carbon-infused media for odor control
  • PTFE membrane lamination for fine particle capture
  • Complete material traceability system
