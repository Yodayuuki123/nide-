## About Us 页面内容

### 公司简介

Philite® is committed to providing first grade filter solution, after years of development, we have established close business relationship with our customers in more than 100 countries and regions in the world. The key to our rapid growth and development comes from our management's ethos of premium quality, innovation and being customer-focused. Our company is a leader in filter material industry, and is one of the top 5 suppliers of air slide belts, filter bags and needle punched felt in Europe.

Philite® actively participates in the global supply chain service market, and we are committed to providing customers with high-quality products and services to promote local economic development. At the same time, we adhere to the concept of sustainable development and fulfill our environmental and social responsibilities.

### 四大核心优势

**1. Quality Assurance（质量保证）**

We have established a strict quality management system. Every batch of products has passed through multiple inspection systems. At the same time, we invest a lot of money every year in the research and development of new filtration solutions to meet the new needs of customers.

**2. Technical Support（技术支持）**

A lot of clients are confused about how to choose the right fabric and accessories, and feel helpless about lowering the cost of bag filtration. The Philite's engineers, with 20 years of experience, can provide a series of consulting, support and advice. All these services are free.

**3. Production Solution（生产方案）**

As a manufacturer, you surely know the higher the efficiency, the lower the costs. We developed two unique technological achievements for our solution to improve the efficiency of the filter bag sewing machine and hot welding machine in 4 long years. Now we are continuing to improve this service for our clients.

**4. One-Stop Purchasing（一站式采购）**

There are so many filtration products, a lot of our clients had troubles during purchasing. We can help you save the time — all you have to do is send us the detail of the products. Especially the filter bag accessories, we suggest you choosing our "one-stop purchasing".

### 服务流程

1. **Design Plan** — We offer a design plan according to your demand of the production.
2. **Configuration Adjustment** — Adjust the configuration until we can ensure the project on the basis of your production base's circumstance and special demands.
3. **Quotation & Final Design** — We can offer a specific quotation and finish final design project through negotiation.
4. **Production** — Starting producing.
5. **Installation & Debugging** — Our engineer will arrive at your production base and start to help install, debug and train employees when our equipment arrives.
6. **Customer Service** — In addition to adequate warranty period, we have prepared a lot of quality supplies. Our sales engineers provide online assistance 7/24.
